Our Work in Action

Empowering communities through compassion, dedication, and meaningful action.

Challenges We Tackle

Breaking barriers to girls' education

Child Marriage

We work to prevent child marriages and ensure girls can complete their education, providing counseling and support to families in vulnerable communities.

Poverty

Financial barriers prevent many girls from attending school. We provide scholarships, school supplies, and resources to help families overcome economic challenges.

Safety and Violence

We create safe pathways and transportation programs while advocating for safer school environments to protect girls from harassment and violence.

Lack of Female Mentors

We support training programs for women teachers and advocate for gender-balanced teaching staff to provide positive role models for girls.

Low Value on Education

Through community engagement and awareness campaigns, we work to change mindsets and demonstrate the transformative power of educating girls.

Sanitation and Privacy

We advocate for and help build gender-separated sanitation facilities in schools, ensuring dignity and safety for adolescent girls.

Digital Divide and STEM

We bridge the digital divide by providing girls with digital literacy training and access to STEM programs, preparing them for future economic opportunities.

Distance to School

We provide transportation solutions and support community schools to reduce travel time and make education accessible for girls in remote areas.

Hidden School Costs

Beyond tuition, we help cover costs of uniforms, books, and supplies, ensuring that no girl is held back from education due to hidden expenses.

Our Long-Term Strategy

Building a sustainable foundation for educational equity

Skill Development

Empowering girls with practical skills and vocational training for future careers and economic independence.

Conducive Learning Environment

Creating safe, inclusive, and supportive spaces that encourage girls to thrive academically and socially.

Mental & Physical Wellbeing

Prioritizing health, nutrition, and mental wellness to ensure girls can focus on their education.

Leadership Values

Fostering confidence, decision-making skills, and leadership qualities to create future change-makers.

Community Engagement

Building partnerships with families and communities to support and sustain girls' education initiatives.

Quality Teaching

Investing in trained educators and modern teaching methods to enhance learning outcomes for all girls.

Where We Work

Puku Didi Foundation started its journey from Sisout, Sarlahi - the ancestral home of Samriddhi. Sarlahi district ranks amongst the lowest in adult literacy (38%) and mean years of schooling (2.42 years). Sisout, a village in Sarlahi, it has the highest poverty rate in the district and has large pockets of underserved communities.

Puku Didi Foundation is dedicated to empowering communities through education, healthcare, and sustainable development initiatives.
